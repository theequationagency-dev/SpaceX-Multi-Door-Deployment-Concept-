## Concept Summary

Proposed replacement of Starship's single-door, single-rail satellite deployment system with a distributed multi-door architecture (3–4 independent doors) positioned at different azimuths around the payload fuselage. Each door operates independently with its own rail, actuator, and satellite staging shelf — eliminating the single point of failure in the current design.
